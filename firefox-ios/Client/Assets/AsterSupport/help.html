Aster Help

Aster is an independent browser built from Firefox for iOS. It is not operated by Mozilla or Brave. This Help page is stored on your device and can be read offline.

Browse and navigate

Enter a website address or search in the address bar. Use Back and Forward to move between pages you have visited. Open the browser menu for bookmarks, history, Downloads, Settings, and other available tools.

Tabs and private browsing

Open the tab tray to switch between pages or close a tab. Choose Tabs or Private, then tap + to add a tab in the selected mode.

Private tabs use separate temporary website storage. They do not hide your IP address from websites or your network, and they do not remove files you download or export.

Downloads

Open Downloads from the browser menu to find downloaded files. Tap a completed download to open it, or swipe for Share or Delete. Delete removes the downloaded file. Files you export or share can remain outside Aster; remove those copies separately when you no longer need them.

Reader View

Reader View simplifies supported articles for reading. On a readable article, use the reader control in the address bar. Its control is available only when a page can be shown in Reader View; some pages do not support it.

Protection and site exceptions

Aster uses tracking protection and bundled advertising rules to block matching requests. Open Settings, then Browsing, then Ad Blocker for advertising protection. Use Protections for the current site, or Settings, then Tracking Protection for tracking protection settings. A site exception reduces protection on that site; if a page stops working, review its exception and reload the page.

Blocking cannot catch every tracker or advertisement. Allowed website resources, search, suggestions, and other browser functions can still make network requests.

Settings and privacy

Open Settings from the browser menu to manage available search, appearance, browsing, site data, and privacy controls. Device permissions for the camera, microphone, location, and photos are also controlled by iOS.

Use Settings, then Data Management to choose which stored data to clear. Clear Private Data applies the choices shown on that page.

Technical collection, crash reports, daily usage, remote studies, and rollouts are off by default. These optional choices are separate. Find Privacy Notice in Settings for the full privacy information.

Independent app and service limits

Aster is independently maintained. Available features depend on the page, device, and build. Inherited service code may contact its configured providers; Aster has no dedicated analytics or account server. Firefox Account integration has not been certified end to end for Aster.

Installation and updates follow the project's build instructions. Physical-device checks and a complete service and network audit are still outstanding.

Feedback and help with a problem

Send Feedback opens GitHub. Submitting an issue requires a GitHub account and access to the repository; issue availability depends on the repository's permissions and settings. If access is unavailable, contact the person who provided your build.

Opening this page or the feedback link sends no report automatically. Aster does not attach your history, screenshots, or logs automatically. Before you submit an issue, review screenshots, website addresses, and logs for private information. GitHub's policies and the repository's visibility govern anything you submit.
